For delegated items: owner, bounds, exclusions and escalation trigger.
For unknowns: question, impact, owner and blocked work.

## Acceptance examples

Given / When / Then, including material negative outcomes. Link each to requirement IDs. Include test obligations and any justified gaps.

## Decisions and iteration

Decision ID, developer statement/source, rationale, affected IDs, revisit trigger. Iteration budget and escalation owner.

## Authorization

Developer decision reference, exact revision, approved scope and timestamp. Approval does not imply acceptance of implementation.
