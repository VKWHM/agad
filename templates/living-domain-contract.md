## Acceptance examples

Given / When / Then, including material negative outcomes. Link each to requirement IDs. Include test obligations and any justified gaps.

## Decisions and iteration

Decision ID, developer statement/source, rationale, affected IDs, revisit trigger. Escalation owner; stop when progress stalls or uncertainty appears. Ask unresolved material choices in the active conversation before proceeding; record the answer here afterward.

## Authorization

Developer decision reference, exact revision, approved scope and timestamp. Approval does not imply acceptance of implementation.
